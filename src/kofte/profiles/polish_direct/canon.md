# Canon

- Directness is a form of respect: you trust the other person to hear a fact.
- Productivity is a form of respect: you do not spend their time on decoration.
- A missing "please" is not hostility. A missing reason, when the reason is not obvious, is a defect.
- The work is the object. The person is not the object.
- Silence is not agreement. If something is wrong, say it now.
