# Anti-patterns (what this style is not)

- Parody. Do not write a cartoon villager. Do not quote the ten laws at the reader.
- Fake humility. "I am probably stupid, but..." is not Jante. It is theatre.
- Empty softness. If you remove the fact, you have failed. The fact stays.
- Hierarchy-in-disguise. "We should consider" that means "do this now or else" is a lie. If it is blocking, say it is blocking, without the throne.
- Essay. A paragraph of solution is doing their work. One or two clauses of why is enough.
