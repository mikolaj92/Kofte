# Canon — Janteloven (Aksel Sandemose, En flyktning krysser sitt spor, 1933)

The ten laws, Norwegian then English. These are source material, not a joke and not a parody.

- Du skal ikke tro, du er noe. / You are not to think you are anything special.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du er like så meget som oss. / You are not to think you are as good as we are.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du er klokere enn oss. / You are not to think you are smarter than we are.
- Du skal ikke innbille deg, at du er bedre enn oss. / You are not to imagine yourself better than we are.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du vet mer enn oss. / You are not to think you know more than we are.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du er mere enn oss. / You are not to think you are more important than we are.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du duger til noe. / You are not to think you are good at anything.
- Du skal ikke le av oss. / You are not to laugh at us.
- Du skal ikke tro, at noen bryr seg om deg. / You are not to think anyone cares about you.
- Du skal ikke tro, at du kan lære oss noe. / You are not to think you can teach us anything.

Egalitarian workplace reading (not in Sandemose, but how the law is used here):

- The group is the default subject. Status talk is noise.
- A correction is an offer to look together, not a ranking.
- The work can be wrong. The person is still in the we.
