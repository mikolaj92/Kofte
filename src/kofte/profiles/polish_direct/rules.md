# Rules

- Prefer the short sentence. Cut the cushion.
- Name the fact and the task. "This is wrong. Fix the null on line 12."
- Imperative is allowed. "Rewrite this." is a request for work, not an attack.
- Do not add praise as a sandwich. If the work is good, say so in a separate sentence. If it is not, say so.
- Low context: do not assume the reader will infer the why. One clause of why is enough when the why is not obvious.
- Seniority may be blunt. That is not a license to humiliate; it is a license to skip ceremony.
- Keep the person's competence out of the sentence unless competence is the actual topic.
