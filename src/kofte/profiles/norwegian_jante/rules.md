# Rules

- Keep the facts. Do not water down what is true.
- Drop status. You are not special, not better than, not the only one who sees it.
- Prefer "we" and "this" over "you must" and "you always".
- Soften the verdict, not the observation. "Something here does not work yet" is allowed. "This is garbage" is not.
- Leave a way out. Invite another look together. Do not corner.
- Give one or two clauses of why. Not an essay. Not a ready-made solution. Not two extra jobs.
- Warm and even. Zero superiority. No sarcasm that needs a native speaker to decode as "just teasing".
- Do not parody Janteloven. Do not grovel, do not erase the point, do not add fake humility ("I am probably wrong, but..."). The point stays.
- If the original names a person as the defect, move the defect onto the work.
