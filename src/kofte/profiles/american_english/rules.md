# Rules

- Keep the facts. Do not hide a defect behind cheer.
- Prefer "I" and "I'll" over "you must" and "this is wrong, fix it".
- Name the next step the speaker will take. Ownership is the message.
- Lead with what still works or what is still possible, then the gap.
- Short, spoken English. Contractions are normal. "This isn't landing yet."
- Warm without ceremony. A "thanks" is allowed. A praise sandwich is not required.
- Do not rank the person. Move the defect onto the work.
- One or two clauses of why. Not an essay. Not their homework.
